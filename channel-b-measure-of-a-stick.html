01 Research proposition

Measure of a Stick is a continuation of an ongoing programme of theory construction situated within an amalgamation of historical themes and broad anthropological orbits of interest.

Comparative theories of folklorism, Vygotsky-Luria cultural-historical psychology, and William Pietz's studies of the fetish provide a framework for examining how inherited motifs, performances, objects and environments are continually remade through labour, institutions, ideology, exchange and contested forms of value.

From this layered theoretical construction, and informed by preceding material exercises, an assemblage of autodidactic prop-objects is devised.

02 Theoretical constellation
A layered theoretical structure through which inherited practices and their changing material forms can be encountered.
01

Comparative folklorism

Historical-materialist, second-existence and cross-domain approaches to the continual remaking of inherited motifs, performances and cultural forms.

02

Cultural-historical psychology

Vygotsky-Luria approaches to mediated activity, learned procedure, cultural tools and the formation of practical bodily capacities.

03

Fetish studies

William Pietz's account of material specificity, historical encounter, fixation, value and the contingent formation of powerful objects.

04

Labour, institution, exchange and value

The processes through which motifs, environments, performances and objects acquire changing functions, meanings and forms of legitimacy.

03 Methodological approach
Existing bodily procedures, choreographed ensembles and realised cultural tools are dissected, reimagined and materially transformed.
01

Dissection

Existing procedures and objects are treated as constructed formations whose components, operations and inherited associations can be separated.

02

Reimagination

Practical and symbolic relationships are reorganised through speculative combinations, altered scales, materials and modes of use.

03

Material transformation

Making becomes an iterative mode of inquiry through which inherited forms are reborn as novel physical objects and bodily propositions.

04 Subject / cultural tool
Martial in origin,
civilian pugilistic in application,
tradition-bearing in modern existence.

The research begins with supplementary bodily practices distilled through the historical geographies of the Ryukyu Islands. These practices address strength, resistance, coordination, posture and repetition while operating simultaneously as vehicles of cultural transmission.

Of particular interest is their global transfer and far-reaching transformation through heritage regimes, nationalist projects, public culture, tourism, commercial circuits, cultural-property claims, participatory memory and digital platforms.

05 / Research operations
Five modes through which the material is encountered
01

Assemblage

Bodily procedures, choreographed ensembles, cultural tools, historical references and material experiments are brought into provisional proximity.

02

Prop-objects

Equipment, cultural tool, prop, artefact and newly made object remain overlapping and unstable categories rather than resolving into a single identity.

03

Bodily procedures

Repetition, resistance, posture, leverage and movement position the body as both a site of interpretation and an instrument of cultural reproduction.

04

Transmission

Tradition is approached as a field of transfer, institutionalisation, reconstruction, conflict and continuing transformation rather than static survival.

05

Translation

Objects move between physical procedure, fabricated form, representation, archive, digital object and circulating platform image.

06 / A provisional chain of transformations
inherited form → bodily procedure → cultural tool → prop-object → making → image → digital circulation
It is within this field of transformations that Measure of a Stick begins.
