An iterative inquiry through making into inherited bodily procedures, cultural tools and their material, ideological and digital transformations.
01

RESEARCH
PROPOSITION

Measure of a Stick is a continuation of an ongoing programme of theory construction situated within an amalgamation of historical themes and broad anthropological orbits of interest.

It examines how inherited motifs, performances, objects and environments are continually remade through labour, institutions, ideology, exchange and contested forms of value.

04

SUBJECT /
CULTURAL TOOL
Martial in origin,
civilian pugilistic in application,
tradition-bearing in modern existence.

The research begins with supplementary bodily practices distilled through the historical geographies of the Ryukyu Islands. These practices address strength, resistance, coordination, posture and repetition while operating simultaneously as vehicles of cultural transmission.

Of particular interest is their global transfer and far-reaching transformation through heritage regimes, nationalist projects, public culture, tourism, commercial circuits, cultural-property claims, participatory memory and digital platforms.

05 / RESEARCH OPERATIONS
FIVE MODES THROUGH WHICH THE MATERIAL IS ENCOUNTERED
[01]

ASSEMBLAGE

Bodily procedures, choreographed ensembles, cultural tools, historical references and material experiments are brought into provisional proximity.

[02]

PROP-OBJECTS

Equipment, cultural tool, prop, artefact and newly made object remain overlapping and unstable categories rather than resolving into a single identity.

[03]

BODILY PROCEDURES

Repetition, resistance, posture, leverage and movement position the body as both a site of interpretation and an instrument of cultural reproduction.

[04]

TRANSMISSION

Tradition is approached as a field of transfer, institutionalisation, reconstruction, conflict and continuing transformation rather than static survival.

[05]

TRANSLATION

Objects move between physical procedure, fabricated form, representation, archive, digital object and circulating platform image.

06 / A PROVISIONAL CHAIN OF TRANSFORMATIONS
inherited form → bodily procedure → cultural tool → prop-object → making → image → digital circulation
It is within this field of transformations that Measure of a Stick begins.
